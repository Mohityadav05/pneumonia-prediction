PNEUMONIA: GENERAL PRECAUTIONS AND CARE INFORMATION
(Educational reference only — not a substitute for professional medical advice)

WHAT IS PNEUMONIA
Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs. The air
sacs may fill with fluid or pus, causing symptoms such as cough with phlegm, fever,
chills, and difficulty breathing. It can be caused by bacteria, viruses, or fungi.

WARNING SIGNS THAT NEED IMMEDIATE MEDICAL ATTENTION
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath at rest
- Chest pain that worsens with breathing or coughing
- Bluish tint to lips or face
- Confusion or altered mental state (especially in older adults)
- High fever that does not respond to medication
- Coughing up blood

GENERAL PRECAUTIONS DURING RECOVERY
- Complete the full course of any prescribed antibiotics, even if symptoms improve
  early — stopping early can allow the infection to return or promote resistance.
- Rest and avoid strenuous activity until a doctor confirms recovery.
- Stay well hydrated; fluids help thin mucus and ease breathing.
- Avoid smoking and secondhand smoke, which irritate already inflamed lungs.
- Use a humidifier or take warm showers to help loosen mucus.
- Sleep with the head slightly elevated to ease breathing.
- Practice good hand hygiene and cover coughs to avoid spreading infection to others.
- Follow up with a doctor as advised, especially to confirm the infection has
  cleared, since chest X-ray changes can lag behind symptom improvement.

PREVENTION
- Vaccination: pneumococcal vaccine and annual flu vaccine reduce risk, especially
  for children, older adults, and people with chronic conditions.
- Good hygiene: regular handwashing, avoiding close contact with sick individuals.
- Avoid tobacco smoke exposure.
- Manage chronic conditions (diabetes, asthma, COPD) well, as they raise risk.

WHEN TO SEEK EMERGENCY CARE
Anyone experiencing severe shortness of breath, persistent chest pain, confusion,
or a bluish tint to lips/face should seek emergency care immediately rather than
waiting for a scheduled appointment.
